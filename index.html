Turn long videos into daily Shorts.

AI with GeniusSau Clipper is a personal automation tool operated by Saurav Patel. It turns long-form video into vertical, captioned Shorts and publishes them to the AI with GeniusSau YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Runs locally

All processing happens on the operator's own Mac. No video, audio or transcript is uploaded to our servers — there are none.

Google APIs

Uses YouTube Data API v3 only to upload videos and read channel/video statistics for the authorised channel.

You stay in control

Access is granted through Google OAuth and can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

What it does

  • Finds the strongest moments in a long video and cuts them into 20–60 second vertical clips.
  • Adds word-level captions, a hook title and the channel watermark, then renders 9:16 MP4 files locally.
  • Generates a title, description, tags and thumbnail, and uploads the clip to the authorised YouTube channel.
